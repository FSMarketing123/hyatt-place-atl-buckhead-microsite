Investment Highlights

Rare Blank-Slate, Fee-Simple Opportunity in the Buckhead CBD

After 30 years of operation under Hyatt Place and its predecessor brand, with Hyatt serving as manager throughout, the Hotel is now available for acquisition free and clear of existing brand and management encumbrances for the first time. The existing ground lease will terminate upon sale, allowing condominium fee-simple interest in the property to be delivered free and clear.

This rare blank-slate opportunity allows a purchaser to implement a comprehensive renovation, select a new brand, and establish its preferred operating strategy. The Property’s size, infill location, and existing amenity package support a range of premium select-service positioning strategies, subject to brand availability and conversion requirements.

Premier Buckhead Retail Block with Significant Recent Investment

Ideal Position Within Buckhead Place at Peachtree & Piedmont

The Property is located within Buckhead Place at the high-profile intersection of Peachtree and Piedmont Roads, surrounded by 17.8M SF of Class A office space, 2.3 million square feet of super-regional retail at Lenox Square and Phipps Plaza, thousands of luxury residential units, and a deep concentration of corporate headquarters. Guests are within immediate walking distance of restaurants, retail, fitness, and everyday conveniences, including a new Chick-fil-A that opened directly adjacent to the Property in May 2025.

The surrounding area has also benefited from significant reinvestment, including the comprehensive redevelopment and re-tenanting of nearby Buckhead Landing around a new Publix. The growing concentration of dining and retail options has materially improved walkability and supports the Property’s ability to capture higher-rated corporate and leisure demand following renovation. Additionally, MARTA’s Buckhead Station is a five-minute walk from the Property, providing direct access to Midtown, Downtown Atlanta, Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, and many of the city’s largest employment centers and entertainment venues.

Buckhead Business District

Redevelopment Opportunity

The Property’s infill location, existing structure, and position within Buckhead Place provide conversion optionality beyond continued hotel use. New ownership may evaluate residential, senior-living, or other alternative-use strategies that capitalize on the surrounding concentration of retail, dining, healthcare, and employment. The Property’s existing room configuration, common areas, amenity package, and parking access also provide a foundation for a future conversion.

Renovation Upside at an All-In Basis Below Replacement Cost

The property presents an opportunity for a comprehensive renovation to enhance the guest experience and strengthen its competitive positioning. The anticipated all-in basis, including acquisition and renovation costs, is expected to remain well below replacement cost. This basis provides meaningful downside protection while preserving substantial upside through renovation, rebranding, and operational improvement.

No New Hotel Supply

No hotels are currently under construction in the Buckhead CBD, a rarity among urban Sunbelt markets. Four hotels opened in 2021 and one followed in 2022, collectively expanding room inventory by approximately 15%. No new hotels have been delivered since 2022, and with the prior wave of supply now absorbed, existing hotels are positioned to benefit from a highly constrained development pipeline.

Improving Office and Lodging Market Momentum in Buckhead

Buckhead’s premier office assets continue to attract major tenants and investment. Within one mile of the Property, Weinberg Wheeler expanded at 3344 Peachtree, Rayonier selected Terminus 100 for its new headquarters, Greenberg Traurig renewed at Terminus 200, and SouthState Bank established a major presence at Prominence Tower. Also less than one mile from the Property, CP Group’s redevelopment of Piedmont Center is enhancing amenities and attracting popular restaurants and retail tenants. These commitments reflect employers’ and investors’ continued preference for well-located, highly amenitized office buildings in the Buckhead CBD.

Buckhead lodging performance has also improved. YTD RevPAR has increased 6.5% year over year as of July 2026. The combination of strengthening hotel performance and renewed office activity provides a favorable backdrop for the Property’s renovation and repositioning.

Major Future Demand Catalysts

Atlanta will host Super Bowl LXII at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in 2028 and the NCAA Men’s Final Four in 2031. These major events will add to Atlanta’s recurring calendar of professional sports, college football, conventions, and entertainment, supporting citywide lodging demand.

The Property’s proximity to MARTA allows guests to travel directly from Buckhead to Downtown Atlanta, where Mercedes-Benz Stadium and many of the market’s largest event venues are located.
